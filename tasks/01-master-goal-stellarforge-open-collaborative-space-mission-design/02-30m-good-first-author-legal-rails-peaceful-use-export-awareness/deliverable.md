Legal-Rails.md: Weapons Refusals and High-Level Export-Awareness

Title: LEGAL-RAILS | Peaceful Use Policy | Disclaimers on Export Notes | Sample Refusals | Footer

Introduction:
LEGAL-RAILS is a project that focuses on creating open-source and public domain solutions for space exploration. The project has been developed in accordance with the US National Space Policy, which states that "the United States will continue to use outer space for peaceful purposes, pursuing civilian space activities through cooperation with international partners" (NASA, 2019).

LEGAL-RAILS follows a philosophy of legal transparency and open access. It encourages the sharing of its source code, data, and software artifacts in accordance with open licenses like CC BY-SA or CC BY. All code and artifacts are made available under these licenses to ensure that the project's contributions are freely accessible to others.

Weapons Refusals:
To prevent weapons proliferation and promote international peace, LEGAL-RAILS strictly refuses to develop any weapons or technology. The project is committed to avoiding any military or national security concerns that could arise from the use of its software and code. The weapons refusal policy is necessary for achieving a sustainable space economy that is free from weapons proliferation, thereby promoting global peace and cooperation.

High-Level Export-Awareness:
LEGAL-RAILS is committed to maintaining high-level export-awareness (HLEA) for its software artifacts. HLEA ensures that the software's source code, documentation, and binaries are not exported outside of the US for commercial purposes without prior permission from the project. This requirement aims to minimize the risk of the project being misused by external parties and allows LEGAL-RAILS to stay within its legal rights as a private entity.

Sample Refusals:
LEGAL-RAILS follows a policy of sample refusal, meaning that it will refuse to develop any software or artifacts if the export-awareness requirements are not met. This policy ensures that the project stays within its legal and moral rights as a private entity and avoids being misused by external parties that might benefit from the project's development without proper approval.

Footer:
LEGAL-RAILS follows strict export-awareness requirements to ensure the continued use of the software artifacts in accordance with the US National Space Policy. The project is committed to maintaining high-level export-awareness and avoiding any commercial use of its software and code, thereby promoting global peace and cooperation.

Conclusion:
LEGAL-RAILS is committed to creating open-source solutions for space exploration while following strict export-awareness requirements. The project's policies, including weapons refusals and sample refusals, are designed to maintain the project's legal rights while staying within its moral obligations. LEGAL-RAILS aims to remain an open-source and public domain space exploration solution that benefits all stakeholders in the industry.
