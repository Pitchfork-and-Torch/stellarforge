Legal-Rails.md: Weapons Refusals and High-Level Export-Awareness

Title: LEGAL-RAILS | Peaceful Use Policy | Disclaimers on Export Notes | Sample Refusals | Footer

Introduction:
LEGAL-RAILS is the peaceful-use and legal-awareness note for the StellarForge open education kit. The kit is aligned with the stated policy of the United States that "activities in space should be devoted to peaceful purposes for the benefit of all humankind" (National Aeronautics and Space Act of 1958, as codified at 51 U.S.C. 20102(a)).

LEGAL-RAILS follows a philosophy of legal transparency and open access. The kit's code, data, and artifacts are released under the project license, MIT / Apache-2.0 (see LICENSE and NOTICE in the package root). Both licenses let anyone use, copy, modify, redistribute, and sell the material, including for commercial purposes, as long as the copyright and license notices are kept. No permission from the project is needed for any of these uses.

Weapons Refusals:
To prevent weapons proliferation and promote international peace, LEGAL-RAILS strictly refuses to develop any weapons or technology. The project is committed to avoiding any military or national security concerns that could arise from the use of its software and code. The weapons refusal policy is necessary for achieving a sustainable space economy that is free from weapons proliferation, thereby promoting global peace and cooperation.

High-Level Export-Awareness:
Export awareness here is a content rule for contributors. Keep the kit to publicly available, educational material: textbook-level physics, design interfaces, and mission concepts built from public references. Do not add technical data that is controlled under export regimes such as ITAR or the EAR (for example detailed munitions, strike systems, or reentry attack profiles), because that material cannot be released under an open license. Export awareness does not restrict who may download, use, or redistribute the published kit; the MIT / Apache-2.0 license already grants that to everyone. This note is not legal advice.

Sample Refusals:
Peer reviewers refuse contributions that fall outside the rails above: weapons or strike-system design, reentry attack profiles, classified or export-controlled technical data, malware, unauthorized-access tooling, or civilian surveillance products. Refusal is about the content of a contribution, not about who submits it or where they are located.

Footer:
LEGAL-RAILS keeps the kit to peaceful, educational use consistent with the US policy statement quoted above. The kit is open-licensed under MIT / Apache-2.0; commercial use and redistribution are allowed under those terms. This note is not legal advice.

Conclusion:
LEGAL-RAILS is committed to creating open-license solutions for space exploration while keeping contributions free of weapons work and export-controlled technical data. The project's policies, including weapons refusals and sample refusals, keep the kit peaceful and redistributable. LEGAL-RAILS aims to remain an open-licensed (MIT / Apache-2.0) space exploration education kit that benefits all stakeholders in the industry.
